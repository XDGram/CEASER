# Voiceover script (v2, explainer)

About 100 to 110 seconds. The opening tweet montage runs before the first line.
Generate with ai33.pro, ElevenLabs engine, calm clear narrator, speed 1.0.
Best delivered as one clip per block (split at the blank lines). If "CEASER" is
mispronounced, type it as "Caesar" in the audio text only.

```

Here's how it works.

And a safety release date means your bag is never trapped.

If you leave early, you pay a penalty.
That penalty doesn't go to us.
It goes into a community pool, and it's paid to the people who held.
Dumpers pay the diamond hands.

And NEAR is at the core of everything.

No spam. Real conviction.

The NEAR you hold and stake sets your tier.
One NEAR lets you post.

Campaigns need more NEAR as they get bigger.
And twenty-five lets you lead with announcements.
Even communities are ranked by the NEAR their members hold.

To hold, to speak, to lead, or to launch, you use NEAR.
Memecoin activity becomes NEAR demand.

Stop fumbling your moonbag.
CEASER. Built on NEAR.
Try it now. No wallet needed. We'll set you up with test NEAR in seconds.
```
